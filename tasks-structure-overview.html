10  Tasks & Structure Overview

10.1 The City Scan Structure

City Scan is a standardized analytical framework for rapidly generating multi-thematic profiles of cities. Each scan draws on globally available geospatial datasets — satellite imagery, open administrative records, gridded models, and remote APIs — to produce a consistent set of maps, charts, and summary statistics across five thematic domains: population and demographics, economic activity, built form, climate conditions, and risk identification.

The pipeline is organized into discrete tasks, each responsible for a single dataset: downloading, clipping to the city’s area of interest (AOI), processing, and writing standardized outputs to mnt/{scan-id}/02-process-output/ (spatial and tabular data) and 03-render-output/ (maps and charts). Tasks are run independently and share outputs only through well-defined file naming conventions. This design means individual tasks can be updated, replaced, or skipped without affecting unrelated outputs.

This section provides a navigable overview of the documentation. Each chapter below corresponds to a thematic module; each listed item links to its documentation section covering what the layer is, why it is used, how it is produced, and the specific task scripts and output files involved.

10.2 Population & Demographics

Covers population size, spatial distribution, age structure, and relative wealth within the city, drawing on gridded population products, city-level economic databases, and machine learning wealth estimates.

Section Description
Population Growth (Oxford Economics) Annual population time series 2000–2040 at functional urban area level
Population Growth (WorldPop) Gridded annual population totals within the AOI (1km and 100m)
Population Density Current-year 100m raster of residential population counts
Population–Urban Growth Ratio Comparison of population growth rate vs. urban footprint growth rate
Historical Population (GHS-POP) Long-run population rasters in 5-year snapshots from 1975 to 2030
Population Distribution by Age and Sex Age pyramid, dependency ratios, and age-structure time series
Relative Wealth Micro-estimated household wealth index at ~2.4km resolution

10.3 Economic Activity

Covers economic hotspots, growth trends, gridded GDP, sectoral composition, employment, and household income, combining satellite-derived proxies with city-level economic databases.

Section Description
Economic Hotspots Cumulative 10-year nighttime radiance as a proxy for economic concentration
Economic Change Linear trend in nighttime radiance indicating areas of growth or decline
GDP Total & GDP per Capita Gridded PPP-adjusted GDP at ~1km and ~10km resolution
GDP Sectoral Spatially distributed GDP by agriculture, industry, and services (~1km)
GDP Total and Per Capita Growth (Oxford) City-level GDP and GDP per capita time series 2000–2040
Gross Value Added (Oxford) Sectoral GVA shares revealing economic structure and benchmarks
Employment (Oxford) Total employment time series and sectoral employment composition
Household Income (Oxford) Distribution of households across income bands, 2002–2021

10.4 Built Form

Covers urban expansion, land cover composition, road network structure, intersection density, and accessibility to essential services.

Section Description
Urban Extent and Change (WSF) Year of urbanization from 1985 to 2025 (Evolution, Tracker, Harmonized)
Land Cover ESA WorldCover 10m classification into 11 land cover types
Road Network and Orientation OSM road network map and street bearing compass rose
Intersection Density Road intersection count per km² as a measure of network connectivity
Accessibility to Education & Healthcare Network-based isochrones to nearest school and health facility

10.5 Climate Conditions

Covers renewable energy potential, air quality, urban heat, vegetation health, and forest change — key indicators of livability, sustainability, and environmental quality.

Section Description
Solar PV Potential Long-run mean daily PV yield and seasonal availability (kWh/kWp)
Air Quality Annual PM2.5 concentration, local variation, and 10-year trend
Summer Land Surface Temperature Mean summer surface temperature composite 2017–2024 at 30m
Green Space (NDVI) Seasonal vegetation density from Sentinel-2 at 10m
Forests & Deforestation Current forest cover (~2023) and year of tree cover loss (2001–2024)

10.6 Risk Identification

Covers physical hazards including flooding, terrain, geological risks, road criticality, and fire-related conditions — the foundation for understanding where people and assets are most exposed.

Section Description
Flood Events Historical large flood events from the Dartmouth Flood Observatory
Flood Hazards & Exposure (Fathom) Fluvial, pluvial, and coastal flood hazard at three return periods, with population, built-up, infrastructure, and GDP exposure overlays
Elevation and Slope Bare-earth elevation and terrain slope from FABDEM at ~30m
Landslide Susceptibility Five-class susceptibility index from NASA’s heuristic model (~1km)
Earthquake Events Merged USGS + NCEI earthquake catalog within 500km of the city
Liquefaction Susceptibility Soil liquefaction susceptibility from Zhu et al. geospatial model (~1.2km)
Seismic Hazard GEM probabilistic PGA at 475-year return period (~5km)
Road Network Criticality Edge betweenness centrality identifying the most critical road segments
Drought Seasonal NDMI moisture index as a proxy for vegetation water stress
Burnability ESA CCI land cover reclassified to fuel availability scores (0–1)
Historical Burnt Area & Fire Weather Index MODIS fire event history and weekly FWI seasonal profile
